01Arrival & Venue

Everyone should aim to arrive by 3PM on Thursday at Yarnfield Park Training & Conference Centre, Yarnfield, Stone, Staffordshire, ST15 0NL. Registration and check-in follow from 4PM, then a welcome session and dinner.

Yes — the centre has parking facilities and it's free of charge. You don't need to register your car in advance.

02Accommodation & Rooms

All residents staying overnight will sleep at Yarnfield Park Training & Conference Centre itself.

Rooms are allocated by the executives to help you meet new people from other locals and districts — you'll find out your room on arrival day. Rooms are shared 2 people per bedroom, with en-suite bathrooms, bedroom linen, towels, and soap provided.

Keep your room key or camp badge safe at all times once issued.

Tip: don't put key cards behind your phone — this can cause demagnetisation or scanning issues.

03Food

The menu is provided by the facility for the full stay. Please note the venue does not allow food brought in from external sources.

If you have any food allergies, let the district executives know ahead of time.
